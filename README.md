### Hamoud Alshammari

Aerospace engineer in Riyadh. M.S.E. in Aerospace Engineering from UT Austin (2024), where I worked on orbit determination and estimation and then on lunar geodesy and reference frames at the Center for Space Research. Since 2025 I've been at Neo Space Group working on satellite communications, navigation and Earth observation.

I'm applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027. The research I want to do is about uncertainty in space-derived products: when an orbit, gravity field or position fix comes with a covariance, how much should we trust it, and how do we tell when the model behind it is wrong.

Repos worth looking at:

- [basilisk-link-budget](https://github.com/OrbitAR7/basilisk-link-budget) – testing Basilisk's ground-station/downlink modules against an analytic model, plus an RF link budget
